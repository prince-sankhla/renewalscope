Methodology

Know What The Evidence Actually Supports

We classify every finding by the strength of evidence behind it. No invented savings.

Verified
Quote-to-quote difference from written Procore quotes.
Evidence-Backed
Supported by benchmark or public observations.
Potential
Eligible candidate — savings not yet quantifiable.
Unknown
Insufficient evidence to establish a defensible claim.
Example: What We Report
Verified quote-to-quote difference: $16,396/year
⚠ This does not automatically mean removing a specific product saves $16,396.
We explicitly distinguish:
  • Current spend is not savings
  • Benchmark difference is not guaranteed savings
  • Negotiation target is not a predicted vendor quote
  • Unknown dependency does not mean a product is removable
